## Music and sound effects
- **All music and SFX are original and synthesized in code** (no samples, no third-party audio):
  a node DSP script (kick, snare, hats, saw bass, detuned pad, pluck arp, lead, sub "impact" hits,
  noise risers / whooshes, coin chime; Schroeder reverb), A-minor progression Am–F–C–G at 120 BPM.
  Written for this project by the NOXARA team (generated by Claude Code), 2026-11-14.
  License: CC0 1.0 — no attribution required.
- Loudness normalised to -14 LUFS with ffmpeg `loudnorm`.
