# NOXARA: player / UX audit (auditor B: game UX researcher, first-time player)

Date: 2026-11-14. Site: https://playnoxara.vercel.app (mainnet build, banner "LIVE · NEAR MAINNET").
Method: Playwright (Chromium headless) at 1440x900 and 390x844 (isMobile, touch). I played 10 matches with scripted input: 8 practice (`?offline`, Training Island, 390 px) and 2 LIVE (the allowed maximum). I did not sign in with a wallet. Guest creation was blocked while I browsed the menus, so it only happened in the 2 live matches.
Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-zhasulanmedetkhan-Developer-hoard/ea5ce53e-0141-4556-8b3a-38c6a2448fa3/scratchpad/audit/B-shots/` (file names are listed below as `B-shots/<file>`).
Limits: the browser was headless, so I could not hear the sound. My input was scripted (keys + mouse driven from the game state), not a human. Its aim never landed a hit (0 damage in all 10 matches), so "core loop fun" is judged mostly from the screens and the numbers.

## First 60 seconds (narrative, desktop then phone)
0–1 s: a gorgeous low-poly island at dusk and a huge yellow NOXARA, "ONLY ONE WALKS OUT. Take out the richest mercenary. Claim the bounty." This is the strongest first frame among indie hackathon games I've seen; I know what genre this is in 1 s. But the bottom third is 9 empty dark cards and a "CONNECTING…" chip, so for ~5 s it looks half-loaded. At ~5 s the board pops in, and the WANTED card (Okapi, 17.44 NOX bounty, wallets) finally explains the hook.
5–8 s: there are a lot of choices: STANDARD / TRAINING ISLAND / BIG ISLAND / ONLINE soon / 3v3 soon, 5 side buttons, a Bounty Cup with "Prizes announced soon", Sign in. PLAY is unmistakable, so I press it.
Live: "DROPPING IN · sealing the match seed on NEAR…" over the dimmed title for 11–17 s. The first thing the game teaches me is that this is a blockchain thing and that it is slow.
Practice: 3-2-1-GO in ~1 s. I'm on the rim of the island; a translucent arrow, a gold beam crate nearby, "Grab a weapon from a gold beam", WASD/Mouse/Click chips. Good. The top chip says "STORM INCOMING 2:57", so I relax.
10–30 s: nobody attacks me; I wander, see Magpie and another bot fighting in a bush.
30–60 s: HP drips; the white line was the safe zone and I'm outside it. If I met a bot with a blaster, I lost 72 HP in 3 s. Death at ~45–65 s: "#8 ELIMINATED · The storm got you · 0 / 0 / 0:58 · PLAY AGAIN". No money moved, nothing about the bounty, nothing to share. On phone the same happens, with 5 overlays stacked over my character in the first second.

## Top 5 changes for a judge's / player's first match
1. **Fix the storm signposting (B-1):** the chip shows the countdown to the next shrink; the outside-circle warning comes with an arrow and a vignette; first matches get a later storm or spawn inside the circle. This removes most first-match deaths.
2. **Kill the chain spinner (B-2):** a warm-up on the island or a VS intro with the Mark while the seed seals; the chain becomes a small ✓ chip afterwards.
3. **Make the elimination screen pay off (B-3 + B-5):** XP bar, a death-cause tip with the killer's weapon, the bounty still on the board, Challenge-a-friend, and a settlement toast later.
4. **One coaching hint at a time, taught by doing (B-4):** move → shoot → crate → crown, especially on 390 px.
5. **Keep the bounty in view (B-8) and load the board instantly from cache (B-7):** a persistent "THE MARK: Okapi · 17 NOX · 42 m" pill, a gold outline on the carrier, and the board rendered at 0 s with live numbers counting in.
