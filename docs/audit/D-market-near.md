## 3. Scores
- **NEAR depth: 7/10.** Real: keyless bot accounts (0 keys verified), per-guest function-call-key accounts, NEP-366 relayer, settle_match with input_log_hash and result_hash, mainnet contract with public get_economy, 2 standards (NEP-141 full, NEP-245 subset). Minus: no live share market, thin on-chain volume, NEP-245 views missing, hot FullAccess keys, no AI/Intents/Chain Signatures, no one-page proof, stale testnet wording. Crownfall (6,990 txs, proof page) and KEYFALL (Intents, ML-DSA, mainnet) are comparable or deeper in places; NOXARA is the only one among leaders with provably-keyless NPCs on mainnet AND replay-verified settlement.
- **Visual/feel vs leaders: 8.5/10.** Mobile screenshot [verified]: chunky wordmark, Kenney 3D character cards, "WANTED/BOUNTY" poster card, big PLAY, consistent palette, 0 console errors. Clearly ahead of Hearthmarket (text wall), Bluffalo (elegant but static), KEYFALL (flat). Rival: Crownfall 3D realm [assumed comparable]. Minus: desktop overlay transparency, Fighters render glitch (D-10), heavy preload, SOON buttons.
